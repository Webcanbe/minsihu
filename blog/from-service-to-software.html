From Service to Software

WebCanBe started 17 Feb 2026 · major shifts 12 Mar, 10 Apr, 4 Jul

WebCanBe started on 17 February 2026. It did not begin with one perfect definition, and I do not think the later product would have appeared if the first version had been protected from change.

The project went through major direction changes on 12 March, 10 April, and 4 July. Each shift came from finding a deeper repeatable problem underneath the previous version.

Services exposed repetition

Web production made client constraints visible quickly: ownership, speed, responsive implementation, revisions, QA, and handoff. It also exposed how much repeated work sits between design intent and a maintainable deployed project.

The product underneath the service

The question gradually became larger: why are visual editing, source editing, AI modification, preview, deployment, and export usually separated into tools that each treat a different representation as primary?

By the 4 July direction change, the source-owned workspace thesis was taking shape. The real project should remain the object being edited. Visual controls, code editing, and AI should be interfaces to that project rather than separate versions of it.

What survived every pivot

The UI and business model changed. A few principles did not: keep the source portable, make changes inspectable, reduce handoff friction, and avoid hiding important constraints behind “magic.”
