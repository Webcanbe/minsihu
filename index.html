I'm Min Sihoo, founder of WebCanBe.

My work moves between web products, AI systems, operations, and long-form research. I care most about projects where the underlying system can be inspected, owned, and improved instead of hidden behind a polished surface.

This site is an archive of what I'm building, what I've tested, and the ideas that survived long enough to become useful.
