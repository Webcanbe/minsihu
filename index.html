Mathematics · Started 25 Mar 2026

Beal Conjecture research

A long-form attempt to push on the Beal Conjecture while keeping an unusually strict standard for what counts as progress: only a complete general proof or an explicit primitive counterexample counts as a solution.

The research log has covered primary-source theorem audits, exponent and coprimality reductions, Frey-curve and modular directions, multi-Frey methods, effective finiteness, S-unit and height bounds, primitive-divisor arguments, and repeated adversarial checks for hidden assumptions, exceptional primes, and circular reasoning.

Every serious step is classified as known, derived, candidate, refuted, or not yet verified. Failed routes stay in the record so the project becomes more accurate instead of merely looking more successful.

Computer vision · geometry · Started 2 Feb 2026

Vesuvius Challenge

A systems research project around reconstructing and reading carbonized scrolls from volumetric scans: geometry, surface extraction, unwrapping, ink detection, reproducibility, and human-verifiable evidence.

The project is structured around the actual challenge constraints rather than a demo-only model: bounded human input, clean train/prediction separation, preserved character geometry, reproducible pipelines, disclosed artifacts, and inspection of intermediate coordinate states.

The key idea is that the pipeline itself is part of the scientific result. A strong ink model cannot rescue bad surface geometry, and an attractive render is not enough if the underlying mapping cannot be audited.
