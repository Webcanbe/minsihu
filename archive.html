Archive · 2025–2026

Projects, systems, pivots, and research.

This is the fuller record. I keep experiments that ended, systems that changed shape, and research directions that failed alongside the work that continued.

2025

7 Mar 2025 · Personal AI

Jarvis-style personal AI system

Started as a long-term attempt to build an assistant that could move beyond chat. The implementation grew into a multi-stage system: speech input → transcription → model/router → memory/context → queued tool actions → confirmation gates → speech or interface output. Later iterations explored local/cloud model routing and computer-facing tools rather than tying the project to one model.

Dec 2025 · Marketplace concept

Distributed micro-fulfillment

Initial concept for connecting sellers with unused local storage capacity. The idea expanded into a workflow system with seller/host roles, requests, approvals, inventory states, QR movement, and fulfillment handoffs. Implementation work continued into 2026.

Early 2026

30 Jan 2026 · Operations SaaS

Inventory & ordering automation

Worked from real store data toward a system that could detect operational risk rather than merely display stock. The design included inventory synchronization, safety stock, lead-time-aware stockout logic, reorder proposals, and human approval.

2 Feb 2026 · Research

Vesuvius Challenge

Began a research track around virtual unwrapping and ink detection for carbonized scrolls, with emphasis on geometry integrity, reproducible pipelines, intermediate inspection, and the challenge's evidence constraints.

17 Feb 2026 · Company

WebCanBe begins

Started the project that became WebCanBe. It would move through service, marketplace, production, and software-workspace forms rather than staying fixed to the first version.

12 Mar 2026 · Direction change

First major WebCanBe repositioning

The project began moving away from the earliest model toward a more explicit web-production and productized-delivery direction.

Mar 2026 · Commerce services

Shopify store-build experiment

A focused service experiment around creating and shipping storefronts. Useful mainly because it exposed repetitive handoff, implementation, and revision work that later informed product thinking.

25 Mar 2026 · Mathematics

Beal Conjecture research

Started a dedicated research log around the Beal Conjecture. The project developed a strict evidence discipline: primary sources, explicit hypotheses, case reductions, modular/Frey routes, multi-Frey literature, height/S-unit bounds, and a state system distinguishing known results from derived or candidate steps.

10 Apr 2026 · Direction change

Second WebCanBe operating-model shift

Another deliberate repositioning changed how the project thought about delivery, customer workflow, and where repeatable value lived.

Mid 2026

May 2026 · Founder tooling

Startup decision SaaS

Built a working SaaS flow for idea analysis, risk surfacing, execution planning, comparison, and progress tracking. The prototype was built fast enough that market distribution became the next experiment instead of more feature work.

May–Jun 2026 · Commerce workflow

Returns & refunds decision system

Explored a Shopify-oriented return/refund workflow where deterministic rules and AI suggestions were constrained by thresholds and human approval. The useful idea was not “AI refunds”; it was human-in-the-loop decision automation for actions with real cost.

4 Jul 2026 · Direction change

WebCanBe source-owned visual/code direction

The current product thesis began taking shape: the real codebase should remain the primary object while visual editing, direct code editing, AI changes, preview, export, and deployment become different interfaces to the same source.

Jul 2026 · Product architecture

Marketplace → purchase → workspace → edit → deploy

Defined a broader end-to-end product flow where projects could be discovered or acquired and then continue living inside an editable workspace instead of ending at download.

Engineering systems

Aug 2026 · Compatibility engineering

Environment Boundary Map & scanner work

Designed explicit rules for classifying framework/runtime boundaries before allowing aggressive visual edits: server/client boundaries, environment-variable exposure, unsupported patterns, and a scanner-oriented compatibility model instead of pretending all repositories are equally safe to transform.

Aug 2026 · Privileged operations

WebCanBe AdminKey

Built an iOS/Swift control surface for sensitive administrative operations, including manifest validation, operation rendering, and XCTest coverage. The larger idea was to separate privileged actions from ordinary product UI and make them inspectable.

2026 · Authentication hardening

Password reset, passkeys & hostile-input testing

Worked through authentication and security edge cases across Firebase-based flows. Recorded validation passes included a 2,087-test password-reset run and a 940-test emulator pass around passkey work, alongside contract and browser checks for route, secret, and preview-injection boundaries.

2026 · Source reliability

Recovery, portability & deployment workflows

Built and refined Git/GitHub/Vercel workflows around recoverable projects, source ownership, deployment, and failure recovery. One important principle came from real repository recovery work: a project is not truly owned if its current working state cannot be reconstructed when a tool, sync layer, or environment fails.

Distribution & operations

2026 · Outbound system

Research → qualification → personalized send queue

Structured prospecting around researched accounts, verified addresses, source URLs, deduplication, one clear CTA, explicit opt-out, send-state tracking, and deliverability-aware infrastructure. The workflow treated outbound as a data-quality system rather than a mail-merge problem.

2026 · Market experiments

Creators, agencies, founders, B2B events, and acquisition teams

Tested multiple customer frames to learn where production capacity, source ownership, and faster shipping were painful enough to matter. The point of each segment was not to accumulate personas; it was to eliminate weak positioning faster.

A project can be small in revenue or users and still be technically serious. I try to keep the record focused on what was actually built, learned, or ruled out.
